Protect Yourself

Plumbing Scams Older Homeowners Are Targeted With

Knowing the pattern is most of the protection. If any of these happens to you, it is fine to say no and close the door — with us or anyone else.

The Unsolicited Door Knock

Nobody legitimate rings your bell to tell you they happened to notice a plumbing problem. Do not let an uninvited caller into the house to “take a quick look” at your basement or pipes.

Sudden, Enormous Urgency

Real emergencies are obvious — water is escaping. A calm situation reframed as a crisis that must be signed for today is a pressure tactic, not a diagnosis.

The Small Job That Becomes a Repipe

A visit booked for one dripping tap should not end in a recommendation to replace all the plumbing in the house. If that happens, ask to be shown the problem and get a second opinion before agreeing to anything.

No Price Until the End

Refusing to give any figure before starting is the single biggest warning sign. You are entitled to know roughly what something costs before you agree to it.

No Licence, No Company Name

Ask for the company name and whether they are licensed and insured. An unmarked van, cash only, and a vague answer are all reasons to stop the conversation.

Discouraging You From Checking

Anyone who discourages you from calling a family member, getting a second quote, or taking time to think is telling you something important about themselves.

For Family Members

Arranging a Plumber for a Parent

If you are organizing this for a parent, especially from out of town, the most useful thing you can do is be on the first phone call. Tell us what they have described, give us both numbers, and we will keep you in the loop about what we find and what it costs. Many families prefer that the price conversation happens with them rather than at the door.

It also helps to bundle. Older homes accumulate small issues — a seized shut-off valve, a slow bathroom drain, a toilet that runs, a laundry tap that drips — and grouping them into one visit is both cheaper and less disruptive than several separate ones. If a parent's mobility is a factor, a comfort-height toilet or lever-handle taps can be swapped in during the same visit. See our small jobs page for the kind of work that bundles well, and plumbing costs for how visits are priced.

Seniors' Plumbing FAQ

Questions Worth Asking Any Plumber

Ask us these — and ask them of anyone else you are considering. Call 437-996-1996.

Do you offer senior pricing or a discount?

We do not run a fixed discount program you have to qualify for, and we would rather not pretend otherwise. What we do is give you the price for the job before we start, and if you are on a fixed income, say so on the phone — we will tell you the least expensive way to solve the problem, including whether something can safely wait. If a repair would cost more than it is worth, we will say that too.

Can my son or daughter arrange the visit for me?

Yes, and it is very common. A family member can call 437-996-1996, arrange the visit, and handle the price conversation while you are the one at home. Give us both phone numbers and we will keep whoever needs to be informed in the loop.

Will you explain what you are doing before you start?

Yes, in plain language and at whatever pace suits you. You will know what is wrong, what we propose to do, and what it costs before any work begins — and you are welcome to ask us to go over it again.

How do I know a plumber is legitimate?

Ask for the company name and whether they are licensed and insured, and ask for the price before the work. Never let someone who arrived uninvited into your home to inspect plumbing. If anyone pressures you to decide immediately, discourages you from calling family, or turns a small job into a whole-house recommendation, stop and get a second opinion. Those are the reliable warning signs.

Will you come out for one small repair?

Yes. One tap, one toilet, one valve — those are ordinary calls. It is worth walking through the house before you phone and listing everything, because several small jobs in a single visit costs considerably less than booking them separately. See our small jobs page.

What if something happens at night or on the weekend?

Our emergency line is answered 24/7. If water is escaping and you can safely reach the shut-off valve, close it and then call 437-996-1996 — and if you are not sure where the valve is or cannot turn it, call anyway and we will talk you through it. See emergency plumbing.
