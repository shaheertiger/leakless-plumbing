What Professional Means Here

What Separates a Professional Plumber From a Cheap Fix

Anyone can tighten a fitting. These are the things that decide whether a repair holds for a decade or fails next winter.

Licensed and Insured

Licensing means the training and code knowledge are verified; insurance means you are protected if something goes wrong. On any work that could later be inspected or claimed, both matter enormously.

Diagnosis Before Repair

The mark of a professional is finding the actual cause rather than treating the symptom. Clearing a drain that is blocked by a collapsed pipe just guarantees the same call in a month.

Code-Compliant Work

Venting, trap arms, backflow protection, and fixture connections all have rules behind them. Work that ignores code can fail an inspection, complicate a sale, or cause real problems years later.

Proper Materials and Fittings

Right pipe, right fitting, right joint for the application. Mixed metals, unsupported runs, and improvised connections are where cheap repairs quietly go wrong.

A Price You Hear Beforehand

Professional does not mean expensive — it means predictable. You should know what a job costs and what could change that number before anyone starts working.

Tested and Cleaned Up

A finished job gets run, checked under pressure or flow, and left clean. Anyone who packs up without testing the repair is leaving you to discover the problem yourself.

Repair or Replace

When Repair Is Right and When It Is Throwing Money Away

The most valuable thing a professional plumber gives you is an honest answer to one question: is this worth repairing? Plenty of plumbing gets replaced that had years of life left, and plenty gets patched repeatedly when a replacement would have been cheaper by the second visit. Both mistakes cost you money.

Our rule is simple. If the component is sound and the failure is a wearing part — a cartridge, a flapper, a fill valve, a seal, a thermocouple — we repair it. If the underlying item is corroded through, undersized, out of code, or has already been repaired repeatedly, we say so and explain what replacement involves. You will hear the reasoning, not just the recommendation. See our full plumbing services, or read how we approach pricing and cost.

Plumber Services FAQ

Questions About Our Plumbing Services

Not sure whether your job is something we handle? Call 437-996-1996 and describe it — we will tell you straight.

What does a plumber service include?

A standard service call includes diagnosing the problem, explaining what we found and what the options are, quoting the work before starting, completing the repair or installation, testing it, and cleaning up. Parts are part of the quote. If the job needs specialist equipment such as a sewer camera or leak detection gear, we tell you that up front rather than adding it afterward.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. Leakless Plumbing is a licensed and insured plumbing company serving the Greater Toronto Area. That matters most when work is inspected, when a property is sold, or when an insurance claim follows water damage — unlicensed work on supply, drainage, or gas-fired equipment can complicate all three.

What plumbing repairs do you handle most often?

Clogged drains, running and leaking toilets, dripping faucets, leaking supply lines and traps, no hot water, failed sump pumps, and hidden leaks behind walls or ceilings. Beyond everyday repair we also handle sewer line repair, backwater valve installation, and hydro jetting for heavily blocked lines.

Do you serve both homes and businesses?

Yes. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and condo residents through our residential plumbing service, and with restaurants, offices, retail, clinics, and multi-unit buildings through commercial plumbing. Commercial work is often scheduled around business hours to avoid disruption — mention that when you call.

Do I need a professional plumber, or can a handyman do it?

A handyman can reasonably handle a fixture swap or a simple clog. Use a licensed plumber for anything involving your water supply lines, drainage and venting, gas-fired water heaters, backflow protection, or any work that might be inspected or later claimed on insurance. If you are unsure which category your job falls into, call 437-996-1996 and describe it — we will tell you honestly, even when the answer is that you do not need us.

How quickly can you come out?

Emergencies are prioritized and the emergency line is answered 24/7. Non-urgent work is scheduled at a time that suits you, generally within regular hours of Monday to Friday 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Saturday and Sunday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Call 437-996-1996 and we will give you a realistic window rather than an optimistic one.
