Before You Call Anyone

How to Choose a Plumber Near You

The nearest plumber and the right plumber are not always the same. Six things worth checking before you hand someone your address.

Licensed and Insured

Ask directly. A licensed, insured plumber protects you if something goes wrong — and unlicensed work can complicate an insurance claim after water damage.

A Real Person Answers

When water is moving where it should not be, a form submission is useless. If you cannot reach a human quickly, keep calling down your list.

Willing to Talk Price on the Phone

No honest plumber can quote every job sight-unseen, but they should be able to tell you what a job like yours typically involves and costs.

Actually Local to Your Area

A company two regions away still shows up eventually — you just wait longer and may pay for the drive. Ask where the plumber is coming from.

Reviews That Mention the Same Things

One glowing review means little. A pattern — showed up when they said, price matched the quote, explained the work — means a lot.

Explains Before They Start

You should understand what is being done and why before any work begins. A plumber who will not explain the job is a plumber to pass on.

Our service areas page has the full list — and if your city is not on it, call anyway and we will tell you straight whether we can help.

What does a plumber actually do?

Plumbers install, repair, and maintain the water supply, drainage, and venting systems in a building: pipes, drains, toilets, faucets, showers, water heaters, sump pumps, sewer lines, and backflow protection. We handle diagnosis as much as repair — often the hardest part of a job is finding what is actually wrong. See our plumber services page for the full range.

Do I need a licensed plumber for a small job?

For a fixture swap or a simple clog, a competent handyman may manage. For anything touching your water supply lines, drainage, venting, gas-fired water heaters, or work that could ever be inspected or claimed on insurance, use a licensed plumber. Unlicensed work on those systems is where small savings turn into large problems.

What should I do while I wait for the plumber?

If water is escaping, shut off the fixture's valve or the main water shut-off. Keep power away from standing water. Do not keep running a drain that is backing up. Move what you can out of the way of the water. Call 437-996-1996 and we will talk you through exactly what to do for your situation.
